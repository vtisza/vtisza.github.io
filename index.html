About

Scientist, engineer and founder.

The hard part of AI is rarely the model. It's the evaluation, the data, the people, and the decision the model is meant to change.

Today I lead AI Research and AI Engineering, taking generative AI from experiments to products customers depend on. Before that I spent years where models meet money: quantitative research, insurance, retail and enterprise software.

Krónikás

Election forecasts that show the full range of outcomes.

A hierarchical Bayesian model that turns opinion polls into probabilities: each party's likely vote share, the plausible range, and the chance of finishing first. It learns every pollster's lean, and says plainly what polls can't tell you.

Built for Hungary, now forecasting elections around the world.
